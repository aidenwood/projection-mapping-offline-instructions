Operating Principles

  • The internet is not coming. Pre-cache everything, refresh licences at home, disable Wi-Fi at the venue. The network is just the switch.
  • Static IPs, no DHCP. Every device on a known address. Ping before you click.
  • Resolume is the show brain. It owns the projector and the DMX. TD is a feeder.
  • NDI is the only cross-platform bridge. Syphon stays on Mac. Spout stays on Windows. NDI crosses the gap.
  • Map the projector once, lock it forever. Tape the feet, mark the lens, back up the .avc and the .toe.
  • Always have a baked fallback. Live-rendered NDI is wonderful when it works. A pre-rendered MP4 of the same content is your insurance.
